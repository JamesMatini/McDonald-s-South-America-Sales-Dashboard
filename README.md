# McDonald-s-South-America-Sales-Dashboard
This project builds a 2022 sales performance dashboard for McDonald’s South America, analyzing sales trends, profit, customer volume, and satisfaction across countries. It highlights key insights, compares yearly performance, and identifies opportunities for operational and service improvement.

# 🍟 McDonald's South America Sales Performance Dashboard (2022)

## 🎯 Objective  
The **McDonald's South America Sales Performance Dashboard** is built entirely in **Microsoft Excel** to analyze **sales, profit, customer volume, and customer satisfaction** across multiple countries for the year **2022**.  
This dashboard helps the organization understand performance trends, identify growth opportunities, and improve customer experience across South America.

## 🚀 Key Features (Excel-Based)

- **Sales Trends Comparison (2021 vs 2022)**  
  Line charts and Excel formulas compare year-over-year performance.  

- **Monthly Sales Breakdown**  
  Identify the highest and lowest performing months with dynamic charts.  

- **Profit vs Customer Volume Analysis**  
  Explore how profit levels relate to customer numbers using Excel pivot tables and charts.  

- **Country-Level Sales Performance**  
  See which countries contribute the most to total sales using pivot charts.  

- **Customer Satisfaction Dashboard**  
  Visual ratings for Service, Speed, Hygiene, Availability, and Quality.  

- **Correlation Insights**  
  Use Excel formulas and charts to analyze relationships between sales, profit, and customer satisfaction.  

- **Strategic Market Insights**  
  Identify which country shows the strongest potential for future investment.

---

## 📊 Analytical Questions Answered  

1. Compare **sales trends for 2021 vs 2022** on a single chart.  
2. Which months recorded the **highest and lowest sales**?  
3. What is the **profit level for 2022**, and how does it relate to customer volume?  
4. Which countries contributed the most to **total sales**?  
5. How satisfied are customers with service, speed, hygiene, availability, and quality?  
6. Is there a relationship between **country performance** and **customer satisfaction**?  
7. How are **sales, profit, and customer volume** connected?  
8. Which country shows the most potential for **strategic investment**?  

---

## 📈 Key Insights  

- **Total Sales:** $2,544 million  
- **Total Profit:** $890 million  
- **Total Customers Served:** 87 million  
- **Sales Trends:** 2022 consistently outperformed 2021, especially from **August to December**.  
- **Top Countries by Sales:**  
  - 🇧🇷 **Brazil:** 953.3M  
  - 🇦🇷 **Argentina:** 553.2M  
- **Customer Satisfaction Highlights:**  
  - Hygiene: **93%**  
  - Availability: **95%**  
  - Quality: **86%**  
- **Improvement Areas:**  
  - Service: **53%**  
  - Speed: **54%**  

These lower satisfaction areas suggest operational delays that need attention.

---

## 🧠 Final Recommendations  

1. **Strengthen High-Performing Markets**  
   - Prioritize operational improvements and marketing in **Brazil** and **Argentina**, the top revenue drivers.

2. **Improve Speed & Service Efficiency**  
   - Invest in better order handling, staffing schedules, and queue management to improve customer flow.

3. **Leverage Seasonal Sales Peaks**  
   - Since sales surge from **August to December**, increase campaigns, promotions, and stock during this period.

4. **Enhance Customer Experience**  
   - Address low scores in Service and Speed to boost overall satisfaction and repeat customer rates.
